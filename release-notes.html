VisionDeck 1.0

A complete private spatial Mac workspace for Apple Vision Pro, with one uncompromised display free and up to five in Pro.

Spatial workspace

  • Up to five active physical or VisionDeck-provisioned Mac displays as spatial screens.
  • Flat, Curved, and Mixed layouts with direct move, resize, rotation, tilt, and curve controls.
  • Exact screen distance and physical size on both Mac and Vision Pro, including multi-selection in Precision Layout.
  • Saved Workspaces preserve count, aspect, resolution, position, distance, size, rotation, tilt, and curve.
  • Apple system-environment coexistence plus an optional Custom Photo environment.

Clarity and low-latency streaming

  • Hardware HEVC with H.264 fallback, low-latency TCP and UDP transport, bounded queues, and stale-frame rejection.
  • Focused-screen quality allocation with privacy-preserving active-screen and head-direction signals; peripheral screens run lighter.
  • Standard mode uses a latency-calibrated resolution ceiling; optional Maximum Quality lifts it to 4K.
  • Single-pass contrast-adaptive text sharpening on Vision Pro without an extra queued frame.
  • Live stream health for latency, RTT, packet loss, stale frames, decode percentiles, and transport state.

Control, input, and continuity

  • Mac audio, pointer, click, scroll, and keyboard input over the paired connection.
  • Bonjour discovery, explicit code-confirmed pairing, TLS control, and DTLS datagrams.
  • Auto-reconnect after app restart, Mac sleep, or local-network interruption.
  • A deterministic Mac session schematic that shows layout and stream state without a second live 3D renderer.
  • Full first-launch guidance on both devices and signed Sparkle updates on Mac.

VisionDeck Free and Pro

  • Free includes one complete display with the same quality, latency, audio, input, and spatial controls.
  • Pro unlocks two to five displays, Saved Workspaces, Precision Layout, and Custom Photo.
  • Monthly and yearly StoreKit subscriptions share one entitlement, with an eligible 14-day trial, Restore Purchases, Family Sharing, and billing-grace support.
  • When verified Pro access ends, an open session continues; the next session returns to one display without deleting saved setups.

Distribution status

  • Vision Pro app: planned as a free App Store download with optional Pro subscriptions.
  • Mac Host: planned as a free Developer ID-signed and notarized DMG from the official release archive.
  • The source candidate passes the automated development gate; signed production archives and final physical-device qualification remain separate release requirements.

Honest limitations

  • Local network only; there is no cloud relay or internet pairing.
  • One Mac per Vision Pro session.
  • Screen Recording permission is required on Mac.
  • Apple controls its named system-environment picker; public APIs do not let VisionDeck select a specific Apple environment.
