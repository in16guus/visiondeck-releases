One-line description

VisionDesk brings up to five real Mac displays into Apple Vision Pro as a private, precisely configurable spatial workspace.

Product story

VisionDesk is made for people who want the working space of a multi-monitor desk without adding physical monitors. The free Mac Host creates and captures real macOS displays; VisionDesk renders them around the wearer in flat, curved, or mixed layouts. Users can set distance, physical size, rotation, tilt, curve, and saved workspaces while focus-aware streaming prioritizes the active display.

What makes it different

  • One complete display is free; Pro expands to two through five.
  • Real macOS virtual displays, not a static remote-desktop canvas.
  • Local encrypted pairing, no VisionDesk account, no cloud workspace, and no analytics.
  • Mac audio, pointer, keyboard support, and visual controls designed for responsive desktop work.

Long (for feature articles)

VisionDesk is a spatial multi-screen Mac workspace for Apple Vision Pro, built by an independent developer. Its free Mac companion provisions real macOS virtual displays, captures them with ScreenCaptureKit, and streams them with hardware HEVC encoding over the local network to the headset, where they appear as draggable, tiltable, curvable planes arranged in flat, curved, or mixed layouts.

Because the displays are real to macOS, windows, Spaces, and the cursor behave exactly as on physical monitors. Focus-aware streaming spends bandwidth on the display the wearer is working on, keeping text crisp without exhausting the encoder budget, and Mac audio, pointer, and keyboard input round out a complete desk replacement.

VisionDesk includes one complete display for free — full resolution, audio, and input included. VisionDesk Pro ($7.99/month or $59.99/year with a 14-day free trial and Family Sharing) expands the workspace to five displays, adds saved workspaces, Precision Layout, and Custom Photo environments. VisionDesk has no account system, no cloud workspace, and no analytics: the stream never leaves the local network.

Useful review setup

  • Use Apple Vision Pro and an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26.5 or later.
  • Install the App Store app, then the free Mac Host. The setup guide covers pairing and Screen Recording.
  • For a clean test, keep both devices on a private local network; Ethernet to the Mac plus Wi-Fi to Vision Pro is a strong baseline.
  • For a dedicated local wireless link, the optional macOS Internet Sharing setup is documented in the network guide.
  • Use your independent judgement. Test access or product information never obliges coverage, a positive review, or a particular claim.
