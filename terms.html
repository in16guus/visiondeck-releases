License and scope

VisionDeck is licensed under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. These additional terms explain VisionDeck Free, the optional VisionDeck Pro subscription, and the companion Mac Host.

Effective July 12, 2026

VisionDeck Free

The free Vision Pro app provides one complete Mac display with available resolution, audio, input, spatial arrangement controls, visual quality controls, and coexistence with Apple system environments. Streaming quality and latency optimizations are not reserved for Pro.

VisionDeck Pro

VisionDeck Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that unlocks two to five displays, Saved Workspaces, Precision Layout on Mac, and Custom Photo environments. Monthly and yearly plans grant the same feature entitlement.

  • Eligible new subscribers may receive a 14-day free trial.
  • The selected plan is charged to the Apple Account after confirmation.
  • After a trial, the subscription renews at the displayed price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Apple administers trial eligibility, localized pricing, taxes, renewal dates, Family Sharing, billing recovery, cancellation, and refunds.

Manage, restore, or end Pro access

Manage or cancel

Open the VisionDeck Pro sheet and select Manage Subscription, or use Apple Account subscription settings. Restoring a purchase never creates a new charge.

When access ends

A running immersive session is not interrupted solely because a subscription expires. After leaving the workspace or disconnecting, VisionDeck returns to one active display. Saved workspaces and Custom Photos remain stored locally but locked; VisionDeck does not delete them.

The Mac Host remains free

The companion Mac Host is a separately downloaded application required for streaming. Public builds are distributed as a Developer ID-signed and notarized DMG. The Host has no separate purchase or subscription.

Acceptable use and availability

Use VisionDeck only with a Mac you own or are authorized to control. Do not use it to access third-party systems without permission. Features depend on compatible Apple hardware, operating systems, local-network conditions, and App Store availability.

Support and related policies

Use the support guide for product help and the privacy policy for data flows and permissions. Public product issues belong in the support repository; vulnerabilities belong in the private security advisory form.
