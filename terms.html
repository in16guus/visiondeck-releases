VisionDeck Free

The free visionOS app provides one complete Mac display with the available resolution, audio, input, spatial arrangement controls, visual quality controls, and Apple-system-environment coexistence.

VisionDeck Pro

VisionDeck Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that unlocks two to five displays, saved workspaces, and Custom Photo environments. Monthly and yearly plans grant the same feature entitlement.

  • New eligible subscribers may receive a 14-day free trial.
  • The selected plan is charged to the Apple Account after confirmation.
  • After a trial, the subscription renews at the displayed price unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Trial eligibility, local pricing, taxes, renewal dates, Family Sharing, billing recovery, cancellation, and refunds are administered by Apple.

Manage or cancel

Open the VisionDeck Pro sheet and select Manage Subscription, or manage subscriptions from your Apple Account settings. Restoring a purchase never creates a new charge.

When Pro access ends

A running immersive session is not interrupted solely because a subscription expires. After leaving the workspace or disconnecting, VisionDeck returns to one active display. Saved workspaces and Custom Photos remain stored locally but locked; VisionDeck does not delete them.

Mac Host

The companion Mac Host is a free, separately downloaded application required for streaming. It is distributed as a Developer ID-signed and notarized DMG. It does not contain a separate purchase or subscription.

Acceptable use and availability

Use VisionDeck only with a Mac you own or are authorized to control. Do not use it to access third-party systems without permission. Features depend on compatible Apple hardware, operating systems, local-network conditions, and App Store availability.
